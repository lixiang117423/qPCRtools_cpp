## Verified Requirements

The following requirements come from the user-provided 2026-10-15 excerpt:

- Application Notes are limited to 4 pages, approximately 2,600 words or 2,000 words plus one figure.
- Software or data must be freely available to non-commercial users.
- Availability and Implementation must be clearly stated in the article.
- Software must remain available for at least two years after publication.
- Supplementary material is published online only and must be mentioned in the abstract.
- The application name must appear in the title.
