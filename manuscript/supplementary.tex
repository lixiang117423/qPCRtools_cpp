\section{Analysis output on the bundled example data}

{\sloppy Figure~S1 shows the two analyses discussed in the main text, produced from the
application's numerical output by the plotting script shipped in the
repository (\texttt{code/validation/plot\_examples.py}, matplotlib), which
gives finer control over the layout than the in-interface figure export. The
per-group means, standard deviations and $P$-values were independently
reproduced without the application or the R package by
\texttt{code/validation/}\allowbreak
\texttt{check\_example\_stats.R}.\par}

\section{Reproducible re-analysis of published qPCR data}

The two real example plates distributed with the 2022 \textit{qPCRtools}
paper were processed independently in the graphical interface. The
four-gene dilution series was used to demonstrate curve fitting and
quality-control rejection. The separate treatment plate contains six genes,
two groups, four biological replicates and two technical replicates; its
design table supplies the published gene-specific efficiencies. Because
those efficiencies are not all equal to the reference gene, the
efficiency-corrected standard-curve expression path was selected. The
treatment data and dilution series are separate published examples and are
not described as one biological experiment.

\section{Validation against an independent base-R reference}

The application and an independent base-R implementation were run on the same
input files for all three quantification methods. The reference was computed
directly from the raw $C_q$ tables and did not call the R package. Every
resulting quantity was compared pairwise (Figure~S2, Table~S1).

The maximum relative difference over all compared values was
$1.9 \times 10^{-14}$. Numerical agreement establishes consistency with the
specified algorithms, not biological correctness.

For the ANOVA path, the overall ANOVA $P$ value was 0.0034471 and the Tukey
compact letter display was b~/~ab~/~a. Group~0 and group~1 differed
($P_{\mathrm{adj}} = 0.0028$), whereas group~0.5 was not separated from either
($P_{\mathrm{adj}} = 0.052$ and 0.073). The Holm-adjusted Welch comparison for
the 0.5 group was $P = 0.0121$, so its symbol changed from ** to * relative to
the unadjusted value.
